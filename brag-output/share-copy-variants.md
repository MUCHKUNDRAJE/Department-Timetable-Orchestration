### LinkedIn
Excited to unveil **Slotify** — an institutional timetable orchestration system built for the Department of Computer Technology at Yeshwantrao Chavan College of Engineering (YCCE).

Key engineering highlights:
- ⚡ **Constraint Engine**: Real-time conflict detection preventing faculty or room clashes across 80+ weekly slots.
- 🔬 **4-Batch Concurrency**: Synchronized practical lab scheduling (Batches B1–B4) across specialized department labs.
- ⏱️ **Flexible Duration Modes**: Dynamic 1-hour lectures, 2-hour labs, and institutional recess intervals.
- 📄 **Studio Export Suite**: High-fidelity vector PDF generation and color-coded Excel sheets ready for departmental circulation.

Built with Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Node.js/Express, and PostgreSQL.
