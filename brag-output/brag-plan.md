# Brag Plan: Slotify (YCCE Timetable Orchestration System)

## What is this app?
Slotify is a full-stack institutional timetable orchestration system built for the Department of Computer Technology at Yeshwantrao Chavan College of Engineering (YCCE), featuring automated conflict detection, 4-batch synchronized lab coordination, dynamic recess management, and vector-grade PDF/Excel exports.

## The angle
College timetables take faculty weeks of headache, whiteboard drafts, and inevitable clashes between labs, rooms, and professors. Slotify solves the entire departmental scheduling matrix in real-time with zero clashes, 4-batch lab concurrency, and 1-click export to official YCCE departmental formats.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"80+ faculty. 4 concurrent lab batches. 0 scheduling clashes."
A high-contrast opening title card that zooms into the institutional badge of Yeshwantrao Chavan College of Engineering, slamming the reality of college scheduling complexity.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Live Conflict Prevention Engine**: Instant detection preventing faculty overlap and room double-booking in real-time.
- **4-Batch Practical Lab Orchestration**: Seamless 2-hour multi-batch allocation (Batch 1, 2, 3, 4) across concurrent labs with distinct color coding.
- **Dynamic Recess & 1-Click Studio Export**: Automatic institutional recess insertion and vector-grade PDF & color-coded Excel generation ready for the Dean & HOD.

## Outro / punchline
"Built at YCCE. Department of Computer Technology."
The official seal, slot matrix animation, and proud student engineering credit.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Select Department & Class**: Target Class CT-5A (Semester V, Computer Technology).
2. **Assign 4-Batch Practical Lab**: Allocate Concurrent Labs (AI Lab, OS Lab, DB Lab, Networks) with zero collision.
3. **Instant Validation & 1-Click Export**: Conflict check passes (0 clashes), producing the official YCCE institutional timetable sheet.
